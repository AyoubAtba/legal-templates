# Competitor Terms Comparison — FreshBooks and Xero vs. the Chexbill draft

Checked on 2026-10-20 against the live terms: FreshBooks Terms of Service (last updated October 7, 2026, https://www.freshbooks.com/policies/terms-of-service), Xero Terms of Use, US edition (last updated August 3, 2026, https://www.xero.com/us/legal/terms/) and Xero's payments terms (https://www.xero.com/us/legal/terms/payments-terms/). Quotations are from those pages. Purpose: show the attorney that the five contested areas in the Chexbill Terms of Service are in line with market practice for US small-business accounting software.

## Takeaways for counsel

1. **All five areas are market-standard.** Both competitors use fee-based caps, broad consequential-damage exclusions, and customer indemnities. Chexbill's 12-month cap matches Xero; FreshBooks uses only three months, so Chexbill's is customer-friendlier than one peer and equal to the other. The $100 floor is Chexbill's own addition from the template.
2. **Chexbill's arbitration clause is more fully built than either peer's.** Neither competitor's clause names a provider, includes a jury waiver, or offers an opt-out; both rely on a single sentence plus "where allowed by law". The template's detailed JAMS clause (opt-out, informal step, batch rules) tracks what US courts look for when enforcing consumer-style arbitration terms and is the stronger drafting.
3. **State consumer notices and the accessibility commitment appear in neither competitor's terms.** They come from the General Legal template and are harmless, but they are not something these peers do; counsel may prune §13 and §14 for a B2B product.
4. **Two peer provisions worth considering for Chexbill:**
   - Xero's **indemnity for breaching the processor's agreement** (payments terms §32) and its **authorization to recover amounts owed from the payments-partner account balance** (§35). Chexbill §8(v) covers compliance with the Merchant Agreement but does not expressly address breach, and §6.4 relies on the Payment Processor to collect.
   - FreshBooks' **right to charge the card on file for negative payments balances after 30 days** (§8). Chexbill has no equivalent, which may matter for terminal-rental installments billed by Chexbill under §6.4.
5. **Two peer provisions Chexbill already has, which support keeping them:** "no professional advice" (Xero §65, Chexbill §4.6) and "you are responsible for verifying the accuracy of any information… for your legal, tax and compliance obligations" (Xero §11, Chexbill §4.6–4.7). Xero's exclusions list expressly names "legal, tax or accounting compliance issues", comparable to Chexbill's "tax penalties or interest".
6. **Data-loss remedy.** Xero limits data-loss liability to restoring from backups (§63). Chexbill excludes "lost data" outright; counsel may consider whether the Xero formulation is more defensible.
